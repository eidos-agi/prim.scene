# North star

Eidos Video is a process for making videos as **scenes you can keep**.

The shot lives in a scene file. The mp4 is a view of that file. You steer the camera, change one thing, and recapture. When renderers improve, you enhance the same shot without spending inference again.

AI-generated video is the opposite trade. You get unique paint. You do not get controls, steering, a slight tweak you can land, or the same image twice.

This product is not reprint-math versus Imagine. It is not a trailer with house jargon. The public face at video.eidosagi.com exists to show a real ident **and** to make the reuse claim falsifiable (same scene, recapture, no new generate).

Prims are the store. Capture is a forge (`video-3d-forge`), not a prim.
